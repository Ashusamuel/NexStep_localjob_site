# NexStep – Local Job Access Platform for Buea
# Project Overview
NexStep is a modern front-end web platform designed to bridge the gap between job seekers and employers in Buea, Cameroon. The platform provides a centralized digital space where students, graduates, and local businesses can connect easily.
This project was developed as part of the **Web Technology and Administration** course.

# Problem Background
Buea is home to thousands of students and graduates, especially from the University of Buea. Despite having skills and qualifications, many youths struggle to access reliable employment opportunities because:
- Most jobs are shared informally through WhatsApp groups
- There is no centralized local job platform
- Internship opportunities are difficult to find
- Employers struggle to connect with skilled youths
- Entry-level opportunities are limited
NexStep was developed to provide a simple and accessible solution to this problem.

#Project Objectives
## General Objective
To design and develop a web-based local job platform for Buea.

## Specific Objectives
- Allow users to browse local jobs
- Enable employers to post job opportunities
- Provide an online application interface
- Improve access to internships and entry-level jobs
- Promote local employment opportunities in Buea
